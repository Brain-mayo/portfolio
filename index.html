What happens on a visit

Keep scrolling to follow one visit from the street to the office.

  1. The worker arrivesThe care worker walks to the client’s home with their own phone.
  2. They scan the door stickerThe signed QR code proves which home they’re at.
  3. Their location is checkedThe phone has to be within 100 m of the client’s address.
  4. The office sees itVerified visits are logged. Anything odd is flagged for review.
